01 — About

Research Focus

I am a Research Associate and Technical AI Lead at UNC Chapel Hill, leading the NIH R01NS125897 grant ($1.24M) focused on epilepsy AI and brain network recovery. My work bridges representation learning for biological images with clinical applications that can improve patient outcomes.

Recognized by Stanford/Elsevier as a Top 2% Scientist Worldwide (2024, 2025), I serve as an IEEE Senior Member, Associate Editor at Scientific Reports and PLOS ONE, and have reviewed over 200 manuscripts across IEEE, Elsevier, Springer, and Nature journals.

My core belief: the most impactful AI systems are those that can extract meaning from the richest, noisiest data we have — the imagery of life itself.

  • Self-supervised & Contrastive Learning SimCLR, MoCo, DINO, MAE for biological image pre-training
  • Vision Transformers ViT & Swin for 3D/4D volumetric image understanding
  • Multimodal Representation Learning Fusing imaging and non-imaging biological data
  • Medical Image Segmentation Instance segmentation & detection on medical imaging data
  • Production ML Systems Large-scale distributed training and deployment

03 — Projects

Featured Work

Featured · Published
fMRI Contrastive Pre-training

SimCLR-based framework learning joint representations from 4D fMRI time-series and clinical measurements. Dual-branch spatiotemporal encoder (3D CNN spatial + 1D CNN temporal), domain-specific fMRI augmentation strategy, and NT-Xent contrastive loss. Achieves state-of-the-art on whole-brain network retrieval and pre/post-surgery classification.
