fMRI Contrastive Pre-training for Brain Network Recovery
SimCLR-based dual-branch spatiotemporal encoder learning joint representations from 4D fMRI time-series and clinical measurements. Domain-specific augmentation strategy with NT-Xent contrastive loss. 07 — Mentorship

For Students & Early Researchers

Across 11 years as faculty, I have supervised doctoral and master's students, guided them through the full arc of research — from formulating questions to publishing in top venues. I believe the most effective mentorship is built on genuine curiosity and honest feedback.

My research philosophy: start with a clinical problem, not a technique. The best AI for healthcare is born from deep collaboration with clinicians, not from algorithmic novelty alone. I actively encourage students to spend time in clinical settings before writing a single line of code.

I currently mentor students through the Neuromatch Academy Impact Scholar Program and am open to research collaborations with motivated Ph.D. students and postdocs in medical AI.
